Privacy Policy

Effective: October 17, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Bobo Learns is a collection of free learning sites run by Babar Ali as a free personal project ("we", "us"). This policy covers bobolearns.com and its subdomains, including woof.bobolearns.com (together, the "Services"). Bobo Learns Chess has its own notice: chess.bobolearns.com/privacy/. We keep it short and plain on purpose. For privacy law purposes, Babar Ali, an individual based in Washington State, USA, is responsible for your data.

1. What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the name and profile details that provider shares with us. If you sign up with email, we store a securely hashed password. We never see or store your Google or Apple password.
  • Sign-in records. When you signed in, and the IP address and browser used, kept by our database provider (Supabase) for security.
  • Learning activity. Lessons you complete, quiz scores, time spent studying, streaks, your daily goal and similar progress data, so we can show your stats across the Services.
  • AI messages. If you use an AI tutor or coach feature, the messages you send are processed to generate replies. Please don't enter sensitive personal information into them.
  • Basic technical data. Standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, used for security and troubleshooting.
  • Messages to us. If you email us, we keep your message and address so we can reply.

2. Bobo Learns Chess

Bobo Learns Chess (chess.bobolearns.com) uses your camera and can use speech features, so it has its own privacy notice, which covers how it handles camera images, speech and fonts: chess.bobolearns.com/privacy/. Where that notice and this policy differ, that notice applies to Chess.

3. Cookies and similar storage

We use a small number of strictly necessary cookies and browser storage. The main one is a sign-in session cookie scoped to .bobolearns.com so that logging in on one Bobo Learns site keeps you logged in on the others. We use local storage to remember preferences such as your daily goal and, if you aren't signed in, in-progress study data on your own device. We do not use advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking cookies. Our Hub and language-app (woof.bobolearns.com) pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.

Guest ID and usage counts

Usage counts are currently off.

When usage counts are on, our server records simple events on bobolearns.com, such as opening a page, starting sign-up or signing in, so we can see what is useful. Most events have a random ID for this browser; sign-up and sign-in events get a separate one-off ID instead, so they can't be linked to you. Each event also has the date, the page area, whether you were signed in, where the visit came from (for example a search engine or a link we posted) and your country.

The random ID, and a made-up name made from it (for example "Guest Brave-Otter-4821"), are kept in this browser's local storage. They are not made from your IP address and mean nothing outside our tools.

We use your IP address only to work out your country. We don't store it in our analytics or guest IDs. Our hosting and database providers keep standard security logs, described above.

Your guest ID isn't linked to your account.

Event records, including the random ID, are kept for 14 months. Daily totals that contain no IDs may be kept longer.

You can turn usage counts off on this device or reset your guest ID with the controls below, or clear this site's data in your browser. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we don't count your visits.

4. How we use information, and our legal bases

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in, and to save your progress and show your stats across the Services. (Performing our agreement with you.)
  • To provide AI features. (Performing our agreement with you.)
  • To keep the Services safe, prevent abuse, fix problems and improve the lessons. (Our legitimate interests.)
  • To reply to you and send essential account emails such as confirmation and password reset links. (Performing our agreement with you, and our legitimate interests.)
  • To meet legal obligations. (Legal obligation.)

We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help us run the Services, and only as needed:

  • Supabase for authentication and database storage.
  • Hostinger for website hosting.
  • Google for Google Fonts and, if you choose, Google sign-in, and Apple if you choose Sign in with Apple.
  • AI providers that process the messages you send to AI features so they can generate responses. These currently include Google and DeepSeek, and may change over time or include others such as Anthropic. DeepSeek processes messages on servers in China. Those providers handle your messages under their own terms and privacy policies, and may keep them for a period. They are not sent to us for any other purpose, so please avoid putting personal details in them.
  • Browser speech services: if you use speaking practice in our language app (woof.bobolearns.com), your browser’s built-in speech recognition and voices are used, so your browser maker (for example Google or Apple) processes that audio under its own terms. We do not record or store your audio.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the Services.

6. Retention

We keep your account and learning data for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep something for legal or security reasons. Backups may take a short additional time to clear.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal data at any time by emailing machinemisha@gmail.com. Please write from the email address on your account. If you can't access it, tell us and we will verify you another way. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EU, UK or a similar region, you also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent where we rely on it, and to complain to your local data protection authority. If you are a California or other U.S. state resident, you have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and we will not discriminate against you for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define the terms. You can also clear cookies in your browser, though you will be signed out.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and database access rules that limit each user to their own data. No online service can guarantee perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

Bobo Learns is a general-audience service for people aged 13 and over (16 and over in the EU and UK). We don’t ask your age, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone younger. If we learn that we have, we delete it. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us at machinemisha@gmail.com.

10. International users and transfers

We are based in the United States. Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries, which may have different data protection laws than yours. If you use the AI chat in our language app, your messages are processed by DeepSeek in China; that happens only when you choose to use the chat and after you agree. When you agree or withdraw, we keep a record of it (the date, the version of the notice, a random ID for this browser and, if you’re signed in, your account; not your IP address or your messages) so we can show what you agreed to. Deleting your account deletes these records.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above, and for significant changes we will make a reasonable effort to let you know.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: machinemisha@gmail.com. Bobo Learns is a project by Babar Ali.
