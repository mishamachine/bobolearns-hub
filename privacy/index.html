Privacy Policy

Effective: October 17, 2026

BoboLearns is a collection of free learning sites run by Babar Ali ("we", "us"). This policy covers bobolearns.com and its subdomains, including lingo.bobolearns.com and chess.bobolearns.com (together, the "Services"). We keep it short and plain on purpose.

1. What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the name and profile details that provider shares with us. If you sign up with email, we store a securely hashed password. We never see or store your Google or Apple password.
  • Learning activity. Lessons you complete, quiz scores, time spent studying, streaks, your daily goal and similar progress data, so we can show your stats across the Services.
  • AI tutor messages. If you use an AI tutor feature, the messages you send are processed to generate replies. Please don't enter sensitive personal information into them.
  • Basic technical data. Standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, used for security and troubleshooting.
  • Messages to us. If you email us, we keep your message and address so we can reply.

2. Cookies and similar storage

We use a small number of strictly necessary cookies and browser storage. The main one is a sign-in session cookie scoped to .bobolearns.com so that logging in on one BoboLearns site keeps you logged in on the others. We use storage to remember preferences such as your daily goal. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To save your progress and show your stats and streaks across the Services.
  • To provide AI tutor features and improve the lessons.
  • To keep the Services safe, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To reply to you and send essential account emails such as confirmation and password reset links.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help us run the Services, and only as needed:

  • Supabase for authentication and database storage.
  • Hostinger for website hosting.
  • Google and Apple if you choose to sign in with them.
  • AI providers (such as Anthropic and Google) that process AI tutor messages to generate responses.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the Services.

5. Retention

We keep your account and learning data for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data, except where we must keep something for legal or security reasons. Backups may take a short time to clear.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal data at any time by emailing hello@babarali.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK or California), you may have additional legal rights, and we will honor them. You can also clear cookies in your browser, though you will be signed out.

7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and database access rules that limit each user to their own data. No online service can guarantee perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International users

We are based in the United States and our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. By using the Services you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in those places.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above, and for significant changes we will make a reasonable effort to let you know.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: hello@babarali.com. BoboLearns is a project by Babar Ali.
