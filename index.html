About

Why this exists.

Good learning resources shouldn't cost a fortune or bury you in notifications. BoboLearns started as a free language course aimed at "occasional speaker" fluency: enough to order dinner, make a friend and get around on a trip.

It's grown into a hub. Languages live at lingo.bobolearns.com, chess at chess.bobolearns.com, and more subjects will follow. The goal is simple: fluency in anything, built from small daily habits.

Bobo is a real dog, and he takes the title of Chief Learning Officer very seriously.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is it really free?
Yes. The learning sites are free to use. There are no ads and we don't sell your data.
Do I need an account?
You can look around without one. An account lets you save progress, keep a streak and see your stats across every site.
How does one login work across all the sites?
When you sign in, a secure session cookie is shared across every bobolearns.com subdomain. Sign in on one site and you're signed in on the rest.
What data do you keep?
Your email, the progress and study time you generate, and your settings. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
Can I suggest a subject?
Please do. Email hello@babarali.com with what you'd like to learn next.
