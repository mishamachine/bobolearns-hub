The manifesto

Knowledge should be free.

Paying to learn the basics is a racket, and we opted out. Learning tools shouldn't hide behind subscriptions, trials and upsells. Access to knowledge belongs to the people, not to whoever can afford the monthly fee.

So everything on BoboLearns is free. No paywalls, no premium tier, no ads, and your data is not the product. That's on purpose.

No paywallsEvery lesson, every site, every feature.
No adsNothing selling you something while you learn.
No data sellingWe keep what's needed to save your progress. That's it.

About

A passion project.

BoboLearns is a personal passion project by Babar Ali. I built it because I wanted it to exist, and I'm sharing it with anyone who might find it useful.

It's not a startup and there's no growth plan. It's a hub for the things I want to learn, built in the open and kept free for anyone who wants to learn them too.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is it really free?
Yes. No paywalls, no subscriptions, no ads. It's a personal project, not a startup chasing revenue.
Why is it free?
Because paying to learn the basics is a racket. Knowledge belongs to the people, so that's how this is built.
Who makes this?
One person: Babar Ali. BoboLearns is a personal passion project, shared for anyone who finds it useful.
Do I need an account?
You can look around without one. An account lets you save progress, keep a streak and see your stats across every site.
How does one login work across all the sites?
When you sign in, a secure session cookie is shared across every bobolearns.com subdomain. Sign in on one site and you're signed in on the rest.
What data do you keep?
Your email, the progress and study time you generate, and your settings. We don't sell it. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
Can I suggest a subject?
Please do. Email hello@babarali.com with what you'd like to learn next.
